01 — About

A portfolio that shows the process, not just the highlight reel

Most junior portfolios only show finished work. This one deliberately shows what I am studying right now, what I actually understand versus what I am still working through, and how my hands-on practice maps back to real SOC responsibilities.

I am a fourth-year Information Technology student at Siam University in Bangkok, working toward a junior Security Operations Center role. My interest sits on the defensive side: log analysis, detection engineering, alert triage, and incident response — the work of noticing that something is wrong and proving it.

So far that has meant an internship at DigiPowerMyanmar, earning a ManageEngine Log360 certification, and building hands-on time with Wazuh and Log360 as my primary SIEM tooling. Alongside that I am writing my own CySA+ CS0-004 study material domain by domain, which is what this repository tracks.

Entries here are only added once the work is actually done. Unfinished sections say so rather than being padded out — if a tool shows as "not started," that is the honest state of it, and it will change as the labs get logged.

Why build in the open? A recruiter can watch this repository mature over time. Commit history is itself evidence of consistency — harder to fake than a bullet list.
